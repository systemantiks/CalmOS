## Walking, riding, jumping and sleep (2.9)

In 2.9.1 all Tofu poses render at 80% of their original size (0.084 source scale,
previously 0.105). Original full-resolution artwork is retained.

Additional assets: `launcher/assets/themes/moose-actions.png` and `launcher/assets/themes/tofu-actions.png`. Created with built-in imagegen. Moose's top four poses are walking; bottom two are sleeping, then two search poses. Tofu's top four poses are jumping; bottom two are riding, then two curled sleeping poses. `PetSprites` decodes atlases at half size and extracts the eight connected silhouettes, retaining only small pixel frames. The cat tree and thought bubbles are drawn on the scene's integer pixel grid.

### Moose action prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Make an additional transparent sprite atlas for the EXACT same Moose German shepherd as the reference: same black-and-tan markings, collar/tag, upright ears, natural proportions, warm crisp pixel-art texture. 1536x1024, 4 columns by 2 rows, EIGHT isolated full-body poses with generous space between them, no overlap or clipping. Top row left to right: four successive frames of a SLOW WALK to the RIGHT (not a run, torso level, paws near the ground, one lifted paw at a time). Moose is gently looking around for his cat friend. Bottom row left to right: 1) Moose LYING ASLEEP with eyes closed and head resting on his forepaws; 2) almost identical sleeping pose, slight breathing change; 3) Moose standing facing right, head turned back over his shoulder looking behind him; 4) Moose standing facing right looking down and sniffing ahead. Maintain same anatomical body scale across all eight poses; each silhouette no wider than 330 px, entire paws, tail, ears inside its cell. Same camera side view and torso height for top row and standing bottom poses. Only dog sprites, no cat, no bed, no ground, no text, no Z letters, no symbols, no shadow, no glow, no gridlines. True transparent background.
